🎮 How to play

  1. Create a round — call create_round("YYYY-MM-DD") for the day you want to bet on (usually tomorrow). One round per date, shared by everyone.
  2. Place your wager — call place_wager("YYYY-MM-DD", true) if you think it will rain, or false if you think it will stay dry. One wager per player per round.
  3. Resolve the round — after the date has passed, anyone calls resolve_round("YYYY-MM-DD"). The contract fetches Dhaka's real rainfall from Open-Meteo and the validators must agree on the result.
  4. Claim your result — call claim_result("YYYY-MM-DD"). A correct prediction earns 1 point and extends your winning streak; a wrong one resets your streak to zero. Check the leaderboard with get_leaderboard().

Rainy day = at least 0.1 mm of rain recorded for Dhaka on that date.

🧪 Demo commands

Deployment runs from the project folder on a PC with the GenLayer CLI. (Tomorrow's date is filled in automatically.)

genlayer network set studionet
genlayer deploy --contract contracts/weather_wager.py

Example round for tomorrow (…)

create_round("YYYY-MM-DD")          // open the round
place_wager("YYYY-MM-DD", true)   // true = rain, false = dry
resolve_round("YYYY-MM-DD")       // after the day has passed
claim_result("YYYY-MM-DD")        // collect your point
